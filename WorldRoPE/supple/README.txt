INTERACTIVE SUPPLEMENTARY MATERIAL

Extract the entire archive and open 3d_viewer.html in a modern WebGL 2 browser.
No installation, local server, or Internet connection is required.

The explorer contains 10 RE10K scenes, each with 6 captured Transformer
layers and 4 Gaussian primitives per key patch.

Select a scene on the left and a layer below the viewer. Each scene
opens in Query View at Layer 4. Query View and Key View align the camera;
the rectangular outline marks the original 256 x 256 image bounds.
Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, or Shift + drag to pan. The image outline
is hidden away from a captured camera. Arrow keys and +/- provide
keyboard controls. Reset restores the overview orbit.

PLY exports include all original supports for the selected layer.
Predicted supports outside or behind the camera may not be visible.
RGB is used for inspection, not as a fitted novel-view synthesis result.

Paper figures & results opens the animation and selected RE10K results.
The ? button opens viewer controls and explanatory notes.

Scenes are numbered Scene 01-10. Each scene has its own local payload file.
Keep the complete assets folder.
