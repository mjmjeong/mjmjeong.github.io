About Viscoelastic in PhysGaia

The viscoelastic in PhysGaia dataset represents the viscoelastic class, capturing the deformation, stretching, and recovery of soft, rubber-like materials under various physical interactions.

Physical Properties

  • Elasticity: Ability to stretch and return to original shape
  • Viscosity: Resistance to flow and deformation
  • Deformation: Bending, twisting, and compressing
  • Interaction: Pulling, squishing, and bouncing
