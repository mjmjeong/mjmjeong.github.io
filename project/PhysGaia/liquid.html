About Liquid in PhysGaia

The liquid in PhysGaia dataset represents the liquid class, capturing the dynamic flow, splashing, and surface tension effects of water-like materials under various physical interactions.

Physical Properties

  • Fluidity: Ability to flow and conform to container shapes
  • Surface Tension: Formation of droplets and waves
  • Transparency: Light transmission and refraction
  • Interaction: Splashing, merging, and separation
