About Gas in PhysGaia

The gas in PhysGaia dataset represents the gas class, capturing the movement, diffusion, and turbulence of smoke-like or vaporous materials under various physical interactions.

Physical Properties

  • Diffusion: Spreading and mixing in air
  • Turbulence: Swirling and chaotic motion
  • Transparency: Partial visibility and light scattering
  • Interaction: Dispersal, merging, and dissipation
